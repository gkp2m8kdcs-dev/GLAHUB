Quick help

Getting started

Open the Markets tab, tap any instrument, and start exploring charts and fundamentals. Add trades from the Trade tab.

Attach a receipt

In the Trade form, tap the photo area to use the camera or pick from the photo library. Images are saved with the trade.

Set a price alert

Open any instrument → tap the bell icon → choose above/below threshold. Alerts are evaluated continuously while the app is open.

Reset the app

Go to Profile → Settings → Reset to clear all portfolios, trades, alerts, and images stored on this device.

Frequently asked questions

Does GLAHUB connect to my brokerage?

No. GLAHUB is an offline-first learning sandbox. There is no live broker connection and no real money is exchanged.

Where is my data stored?

All of your data — portfolios, trades, alerts, notes, and attached images — is stored locally on your iOS device and never sent to a server.

Why are prices updating on their own?

GLAHUB uses a simulated price engine so the experience feels alive. Prices are not real market data and cannot be used for trading.

How do I delete my data?

Open Profile → Settings → Reset, or simply uninstall the app. Both methods remove all data stored on the device.

The camera/photo permission was denied. How do I change it?

Go to iOS Settings → GLAHUB → toggle Camera and/or Photos. You can re-enable permissions at any time.

Can I export my trade journal?

Use the share/export options inside the Trade and Portfolio modules to save or share generated reports.

I found a bug. Where do I report it?

Email jonesberlin9@gmail.com with steps to reproduce, your device model, and iOS version. Screenshots are very helpful.

Is GLAHUB investment advice?

No. GLAHUB is for education and simulation only. It is not a brokerage and does not provide personalised investment recommendations.
