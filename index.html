Frequently asked questions

Does GLAHUB connect to a live broker?

No. GLAHUB is a learning sandbox. There is no account, no live order routing, and no real money involved.

Is any of my data sent off my device?

No. All portfolio, trade, and watchlist data stays on your device. See our privacy policy for details.

Which markets can I explore?

United States, Hong Kong, China A-shares, Japan, Europe, the United Kingdom, and major cryptocurrencies.
