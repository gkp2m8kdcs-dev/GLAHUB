Privacy Policy

Your privacy matters. This policy explains what data GLAHUB collects, stores, and shares — and what it never does.

Last updated: September 2, 2026 · Effective immediately

Short version: GLAHUB does not collect personal data. All portfolios, trades, alerts, and notes are stored locally on your device. The app does not connect to a live broker and requires no login.

1. Information We Do Not Collect

GLAHUB is designed as an offline-first learning tool. We do not collect, transmit, or sell:

  • Personal identifiers (name, email, phone number, address)
  • Account credentials or authentication tokens
  • Trading activity or order history
  • Watchlists, price alerts, portfolio composition, or notes
  • Device identifiers, advertising IDs, or advertising profiles
  • Location, contacts, calendars, or other device data outside the permissions you grant

2. Data Stored Locally on Your Device

The following data is created by you and stored only on your iOS device. It never leaves your device unless you explicitly export or share it:

  • Watchlists, saved instruments, and viewing preferences
  • Simulated trades, notes, statuses, and journal entries
  • Portfolio holdings, cost basis, and P/L calculations
  • Price alert thresholds and trigger history
  • Educational news read status and bookmarks
  • Currency preferences and calculator inputs

Images you attach to trades are stored in the app's local sandbox and are accessible only to GLAHUB on your device.

3. Permissions You May Grant

GLAHUB requests only the system permissions needed for features you choose to use. You can decline or revoke these at any time in iOS Settings:

  • Camera — to capture photos of trade receipts or documents you choose to attach.
  • Photo Library (read) — to attach existing photos to trade records.
  • Photo Library (write) — to save generated charts or reports you choose to export.
  • Notifications — to deliver in-app price alerts you configure.

GLAHUB does not access these resources until you initiate the corresponding action, and the content remains on your device.

4. Third-Party Services

GLAHUB does not integrate third-party analytics SDKs, advertising networks, or crash reporters that transmit your usage data. If we change this in the future, we will update this policy and request any required consent.

5. Children's Privacy

GLAHUB is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children. Because we do not collect any personal data at all, this policy applies equally to all users.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

Because your data lives only on your device, retention is controlled by you. Uninstalling the app removes all locally stored data. You can also reset the app from the in-app Settings menu, which clears all portfolios, trades, alerts, and images.

7. Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction (for example, the EEA, UK, California, or other regions), you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or port personal data. Because GLAHUB does not hold personal data on its servers, these rights are exercised simply by managing the data on your device. If you have a request we can help with, contact us at jonesberlin9@gmail.com.

8. Security

Data is protected by iOS sandboxing and your device passcode, Face ID, or Touch ID. We recommend keeping your device operating system up to date and using a device passcode.

9. International Transfers

Because we do not collect personal data from the app, there are no international data transfers initiated by GLAHUB. Should this change, we will revise this policy and provide the required notices.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date above and, where appropriate, surfaced inside the app. Continued use of GLAHUB after an update indicates acceptance of the revised policy.
